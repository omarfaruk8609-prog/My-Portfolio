A focused practice

I moved away from general pain and stroke care to concentrate on neurological rehabilitation, with Parkinson's disease at its centre.

Parkinson's changes how a person moves, balances, speaks and walks, and it changes over time. Care that works is specific, closely supervised and adjusted at every stage. I limit the number of patients I take on so that each person and their family receive that level of attention.

Every plan is built on current clinical evidence and shaped around the patient's own goals, home and routine.
